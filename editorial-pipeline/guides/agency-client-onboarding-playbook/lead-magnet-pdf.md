## Change rule

When a request changes deliverables, timeline, capacity, or responsibility, agree one of three outcomes before execution:

1. **Replace** - remove or reduce an existing item.
2. **Extend** - move the timeline to create capacity.
3. **Add** - approve additional work commercially.

# 5. Kickoff Meeting Agenda - 35 Minutes

## 0-5 min - Why this engagement exists

- Why now?
- What changed before this engagement started?
- Which outcome matters most?

## 5-12 min - Scope and responsibilities

- Confirm included work
- Confirm exclusions
- Confirm client responsibilities
- Confirm agency responsibilities

## 12-20 min - Information and access

- Review unresolved prerequisites
- Confirm owner for each missing item
- Confirm which items block the first milestone

## 20-27 min - Communication and approvals

- Primary communication channel
- Update cadence
- Final approval owner
- Expected feedback window
- Escalation route

## 27-33 min - First milestone

- First visible output
- Owner
- Due date
- Dependencies
- What happens after it

## 33-35 min - Close

Repeat the next three actions, owners, and dates.

# 7. Internal Agency Handoff Checklist

Before delivery begins, confirm:

- [ ] Approved proposal / agreement is accessible
- [ ] Client problem is summarized in plain language
- [ ] Desired outcome is clear
- [ ] Included scope is clear
- [ ] Exclusions are clear
- [ ] Commercial terms are understood
- [ ] Client responsibilities are visible
- [ ] Agency responsibilities are assigned
- [ ] Final approval owner is identified
- [ ] Important promises made during sales are documented
- [ ] Required access has been requested
- [ ] Known risks / dependencies are documented
- [ ] First milestone has an owner and due date
- [ ] Communication cadence is scheduled

**Handoff test:**

If the salesperson disappeared for two weeks, would the delivery team still understand what was sold, why it matters, and what happens next?

If not, the handoff is incomplete.

# 8. 30-Day Onboarding Roadmap

## Day 0 - Agreement and welcome

- Confirm scope and commercials
- Confirm owners
- Send welcome email
- Create internal engagement brief

## Days 1-3 - Intake and access

- Collect essential business context
- Request required access
- Resolve blockers
- Prepare kickoff

## Days 2-5 - Kickoff and setup

- Run kickoff
- Record decisions
- Confirm communication rules
- Confirm first milestone

## Week 1 - First visible progress

- Deliver or configure the first meaningful piece of work
- Send status before the client needs to ask
- Surface blockers immediately

## Week 2 - Structured review

- Review progress against the agreed outcome
- Collect consolidated feedback
- Log material change requests

## Week 3 - Stabilize the operating rhythm

- Confirm recurring update cadence
- Fix recurring access / approval bottlenecks
- Clarify responsibility gaps

## Week 4 - Onboarding retrospective

Ask internally:

- What created avoidable back-and-forth?
- What did the client ask twice?
- Which access should have been collected earlier?
- Which responsibility was unclear?
- Which question in the intake added no value?
- Which step should become a reusable automation or template?

Then update the SOP before the next client arrives.
