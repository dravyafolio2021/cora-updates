# Cora Studio - Build In Public Strategy

This skill defines the content creation blueprint, video scripting frameworks, and visual presentation systems for Cora Studio's Build In Public campaign. It is optimized for producing high-retention video content (YouTube, Reels, TikTok) targeted at real estate business owners in India.

---

## 1. Core Narrative & Framing

Our content must never be framed as "how to build a website." Instead, frame it as **operational efficiency, subscription optimization, and lead conversion velocity**:

*   **The Problem:** Indian real estate agencies are bleeding money in two places:
    1.  **Software Bloat:** Paying ₹8,000–₹15,000/month for fragmented tools (Typeform, Calendly, separate hosting, bulk SMS, WhatsApp routing, and generic CRM).
    2.  **Lead Leakage:** Slow speed-to-lead times (it takes hours to manually copy lead info from listing portals to agents, during which the client goes cold).
*   **The Angle:** Consolidating your entire real estate operation (CRM, GST billing, KYC storage, automated WhatsApp dispatch, and listing pages) into a single, high-performance workspace that cuts cost and accelerates deal cycles.
*   **The Hook:** Saving ₹1,00,000 annually on tool bills while securing a 3x faster response rate to convert hot portal leads.
*   **The Visual Style:** Monochromatic, minimal, premium Apple/Notion/Claude-inspired design aesthetic. Warm cream backgrounds (`#FBFaf7` or `#F9F6F0`), thin slate borders, clean SVG icons, and zero browser-native alert clutter.

## 3. Indian Agency Tech & Cost Audit Structure

### Current Subscription Overhead (Standard Indian Agency)
*   **Form Builders (e.g. Typeform / WPForms Pro):** ~₹1,500/mo
*   **Scheduling Tools (e.g. Calendly Pro):** ~₹1,200/mo
*   **Storage & Backups (Google Drive / Dropbox):** ~₹650/mo
*   **CRM & Lead Management:** ~₹3,500/mo
*   **SMS & WhatsApp Dispatch API (Msg91 / Twilio):** ~₹1,500/mo
*   *Total current stack cost:* **₹8,350/month (plus development and hosting setup fees)**

### Cora's Value Proposition
*   **Consolidated Pricing:** ₹2,000/month (all-in-one).
*   **No Multi-Tool Latency:** Faster response speed = higher conversion per lead cycle.
*   **Headless Visual Canvas:** Keeps page rendering under 1.2s for mobile ads, maximizing marketing execution efficiency.

## 4. Visual Assets & Production Rules

When creating B-rolls, slides, or UI components for presentations:
*   **Theme Background:** Warm cream (`#FBFaf7`) or warm gray.
*   **Containers:** Elegant card overlays, thin border lines, system-ui sans-serif fonts.
*   **White-label Rule:** Never show default Elementor panels or WordPress headers. Always render the custom, minimalist Cora UI wrapper to preserve premium branding.
