# Real Estate Agency Tech Stack & Tools List (India 2026)
Focus: Gurgaon, Mumbai, and Tier-1 Metros

This document lists the exact features required by Indian real estate agencies, the tools they use, and 2-3 options per category ranging from Premium software to "Jugadu" (frugal/workaround) methods.

### 1. Lead CRM & Pipeline Management
*Requirement: Capturing leads from portals, assigning them to agents, tracking follow-ups, and managing the sales pipeline.*
- **Premium Option**: Sell.do (~₹2,500/user/mo) or Zoho CRM Plus (~₹1,800/user/mo)
- **Mid-Tier Option**: TeleCRM (~₹599/user/mo) or SolidPerformers CRM (~₹600/user/mo)
- **Jugadu/Budget Option**: Google Sheets + WhatsApp Web (Free, but highly manual, unscalable, and leads often leak or get stolen)

### 2. Field Agent Tracking & Attendance
*Requirement: Verifying if field agents are actually visiting the property sites or meeting clients, rather than sitting at home.*
- **Premium Option**: Keka HR (~₹6,999 base + per user) or Darwinbox (Custom Enterprise Pricing)
- **Mid-Tier Option**: FieldAssist or Locus (~₹500 - ₹1,000/user/mo)
- **Jugadu/Budget Option**: WhatsApp "Share Live Location" on agency groups (Free, but easily spoofable with fake GPS apps and impossible to audit historically)

### 3. WhatsApp Automation & Bulk Messaging
*Requirement: Sending automated property brochures, site visit confirmations, and bulk project updates to leads.*
- **Premium Option**: Wati (~₹2,499/mo base + Meta per-message charges)
- **Mid-Tier Option**: Interakt (~₹999/mo) or AiSensy (~₹1,500/mo)
- **Jugadu/Budget Option**: WhatsApp Business App Broadcast Lists (Free, but limited to 256 contacts who must have saved the number) or Unofficial Chrome Extension WA Senders (₹1,000 one-time, but carries a 90% risk of the number getting permanently banned by Meta)

### 4. Media Storage & Client Presentation
*Requirement: Storing heavy 4K property videos/photos and sharing them with high-ticket clients without cluttering their phones.*
- **Premium Option**: Google Workspace Enterprise (~₹1,300/user/mo) or Dropbox Business (~₹1,200/user/mo)
- **Mid-Tier Option**: Google One 2TB plan (~₹650/mo, shared unsecurely across the whole team)
- **Jugadu/Budget Option**: Creating multiple free Gmail accounts for 15GB Drive space (Free, but completely unmanageable), or dumping files in private Telegram Channels (Free, but looks very unprofessional to a buyer purchasing a ₹5 Crore property)

### 5. Marketing Design & Social Media Scheduling
*Requirement: Designing property flyers, project updates, and posting them regularly on Instagram, Facebook, and Google Business.*
- **Premium Option**: Canva Pro (~₹3,999/year) + Hootsuite/Buffer (~₹1,500/mo)
- **Mid-Tier Option**: Canva Pro (Single shared account) + Meta Business Suite (Free for scheduling)
- **Jugadu/Budget Option**: Free Canva version + manual posting from the broker's phone (Free, but takes 1-2 hours daily, looks generic, and lacks brand consistency)

### 6. Listing & Lead Generation Portals
*Requirement: Acquiring fresh buyer and seller inquiries daily.*
- **Premium Option**: 99Acres / MagicBricks Premium or Platinum Packages (~₹15,000 - ₹50,000+/mo depending on city, micro-market, and visibility)
- **Mid-Tier Option**: Housing.com or NoBroker basic packages (~₹5,000/mo)
- **Jugadu/Budget Option**: Facebook Marketplace, local WhatsApp Broker Groups, and OLX free listings (Free, but generates 90% junk/unqualified leads that waste the team's time)
