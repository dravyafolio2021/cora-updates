1. Lead Management & CRM
Highest Data Leakage Area
FunctionTools Used CurrentlyEst. CostThe Operational Leakage (The Real Problem)
Lead capture (portals)99acres, MagicBricks, Housing.com, NoBroker₹20k - ₹50k /mo
Leads arrive in 4 separate portal dashboards. Zero central visibility for the owner.
Lead capture (ads)Meta Lead Forms → Excel / manual WhatsApp noteAd Budget + Manual Labour
Delayed follow-ups by 4-6 hours. Excel sheets get messy and leads go cold.
CRM SystemLeadSquared, Zoho CRM, or just Google Sheets₹1,500 /user/mo or Free
Agents hate filling complex CRMs. When using Google Sheets, data gets copied and stolen when an agent quits.
Lead assignmentWhatsApp message / verbal briefingFree
"Kisne kisko call kiya?" No audit trail or accountability.
Follow-up trackingSticky notes, Excel, or personal memoryFree
70% of hot leads are lost after the 2nd follow-up due to lack of reminders.
