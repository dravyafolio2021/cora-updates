Market Research Comparison: Gurgaon/Mumbai Agency Tool Expenses vs. Cora Features.

Features Required by Indian Agencies & Current Costs (10-User Team) Our Implementation Response (Planned in Cora)
1. Property Database & Custom Filters
Agencies need a dedicated system to store property listings with localized sub-localities (e.g. Sector 62 Noida, DLF Phase 5 Gurgaon), carpet area, configuration, and price tags.
Current Cost: ~₹25,000/mo via Sell.do CRM (₹2,500/user) 		Planned
Custom Property Directory
A clean, structured database layout holding listing parameters, prices in Lakhs/Crores, carpet area, BHK counts, and local search filters.
2. Lead Capture & CRM Storage
Inbound client inquiries from property detail pages must be captured instantly and assigned to agents without manual copy-paste.
Current Cost: Included in Sell.do CRM Suite / Custom Monday.com 		Planned
Unified Lead Router
Inquiry forms placed directly on listings that automatically catalog leads and log their intent inside the dashboard.
3. Verified Field Agent Check-Ins
Gurgaon/Mumbai agency owners need to verify if field agents are actually present at property listings during client site showings.
Current Cost: ~₹7,000/mo via Keka HR (Base fee for employee GPS tracking) 		Planned
GPS Geotagged Check-In
A field verification system that compares the agent's real-time GPS location coordinates with the property listing coordinates.
4. Automated WhatsApp Client Notifications
Clients want immediate updates on booking slots, verified showings, or price alerts directly on WhatsApp.
Current Cost: ~₹2,000/mo via AiSensy/Wati Platform Fees + Meta message costs 		Planned
Integrated WhatsApp Webhooks
Automated messaging layer that triggers property details, coordinates, and viewing confirmations directly to clients.
5. AI Listing Marketing Copy & Scheduling
Brokers need to generate marketing descriptions from listing specs and queue/schedule them to Instagram and Google Business Profile.
Current Cost: ~₹2,500/mo via Hootsuite/Buffer + Canva subscriptions 		Planned
AI Marketing Generator & Publisher
A single-click content engine that translates listing metrics into social drafts and publishes them directly.
6. Professional Media Delivery Portals
Sellers and photographers need customized, clean portals to review property photos/videos rather than messy, unbranded Drive links.
Current Cost: ~₹13,000/mo via Google Workspace Enterprise storage (10 users * ₹1,300) 		Planned
Secure Client Presentation Portals
Branded links where clients view staging progress, download high-res files, and check listing stats directly.
Total Estimated Monthly SaaS Expenses for Agencies: ~₹49,500 / month (Nearly ₹6 Lakhs/year!)
